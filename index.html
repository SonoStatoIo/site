Mine is a hybrid profile, built in the field. I started by analysing the Italian power market for top management, then spent eight years managing and optimising ENGIE Italy's power portfolio alongside trading — building forecasting and simulation models for thermal plants and Virtual Power Plant contracts, and automating the team's tools.

Since 2022, in the IS & Digital team of Global Energy Management & Sales, I lead digital-transformation initiatives: from process analysis to the design and release of automation, data-integration and Generative-AI solutions, working with business, IT and external partners.
